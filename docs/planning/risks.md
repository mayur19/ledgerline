# Risk register

Likelihood and impact: Low / Medium / High. Reviewed at every milestone demo. Owners are roles, not people.

| ID   | Risk                                                                                                            | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Owner         | Status                      |
| ---- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- | ------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------- | --------------------------- |
| R-01 | Rounding rules produce differences auditors cannot explain (e.g. per-line conversion drift)                     | Medium     | High   | Group-wise conversion with one half-even rounding per currency side and largest-remainder apportionment (ADR); residue bounded to floor(k/2) minor units and posted to a named FX rounding account; fast-check property tests over random balanced entries | Tech lead     | Mitigated (M1)              |
| R-02 | Concurrent postings create duplicate or missing journal numbers                                                 | Medium     | High   | Counter row per entity/fiscal year updated under row lock inside the posting transaction; parallel-poster test asserts contiguity                                                                                                                          | Tech lead     | Mitigated (M1)              |
| R-03 | A posting commits into a period that is being closed                                                            | Medium     | High   | Posters take FOR SHARE on the period row, close takes FOR UPDATE; deferred trigger re-checks period status at commit; race test                                                                                                                            | Tech lead     | Mitigated (M1)              |
| R-04 | Direct SQL or a future bug edits posted history                                                                 | Low        | High   | Immutability triggers (LL002), deferred balance trigger (LL003), append-only audit (LL001); audit hash chain planned (LL-019)                                                                                                                              | Security lead | Partially mitigated         |
| R-05 | Credential stuffing or password spraying against the login endpoint                                             | High       | High   | IP window, per-email progressive backoff with synchronous reservation, distinct-emails-per-IP cap, IPv6 /64 normalisation, non-enumerating registration; 2FA planned (LL-018)                                                                              | Security lead | Mitigated (M1), 2FA pending |
| R-06 | Throttle state is per process; a second API instance halves protection and an attacker floods the bounded store | Medium     | Medium | Single-instance deployment documented; stores evict least-time-left with a warning log; counters move to Postgres before scaling out (LL-100)                                                                                                              | Platform lead | Open                        |
| R-07 | Misconfigured reverse proxy lets clients spoof X-Forwarded-For                                                  | Medium     | High   | TRUST_PROXY accepts only false, a hop count or IP/CIDR list, rejects `true`, and must be set explicitly in production                                                                                                                                      | Platform lead | Mitigated (M1)              |
| R-08 | Consolidation and NCI math is more complex than estimated (step acquisitions, mid-year ownership changes)       | High       | High   | Scope M5 to fixed ownership per period; worked examples from the controller persona as acceptance tests; spike before LL-064                                                                                                                               | Product owner | Open                        |
| R-09 | OFX/CSV statement formats vary widely between banks                                                             | High       | Medium | Mapping profiles, fixture corpus from pilot banks, tolerant SGML parser, dry-run import                                                                                                                                                                    | Tech lead     | Open                        |
| R-10 | Trial balance performance degrades on large ledgers (direct aggregation over journal_lines)                     | Medium     | Medium | Indexes on (entity, date) and (account); load test LL-102; balance summary tables LL-096 only if needed                                                                                                                                                    | Platform lead | Open                        |
| R-11 | Time-zone handling causes entries near midnight to fall into the wrong period                                   | Medium     | Medium | Entry dates are calendar dates, periods computed in UTC; entity time zone stored canonically for scheduling (LL-086)                                                                                                                                       | Tech lead     | Partially mitigated         |
| R-12 | Backups exist but restores are never tested                                                                     | Medium     | High   | Runbook pg_dump/pg_restore procedure; scheduled restore drill (LL-101)                                                                                                                                                                                     | Platform lead | Open                        |
| R-13 | Segregation of duties is bypassed by users holding multiple roles                                               | Medium     | High   | One role per membership today; approval workflow enforces preparer ≠ approver (LL-081); access scoping per entity (LL-017)                                                                                                                                 | Product owner | Open                        |
| R-14 | Large estimate uncertainty in pending scope (≈1,028 h) delays group features                                    | Medium     | Medium | Milestones ordered by dependency; re-estimate at each demo; cut Could-have items (LL-098, LL-113) first                                                                                                                                                    | Product owner | Open                        |
| R-15 | Rate data entered wrongly (inverted pair) silently misstates functional amounts                                 | Medium     | High   | Rates stored with explicit direction; preview shows the applied rate; import validation and sanity bands planned (LL-051)                                                                                                                                  | Product owner | Partially mitigated         |
